How predictions are made
StepWhat it does
1. MarketBookmaker 1X2 odds are de-vigged (power method) into fair probabilities. This is the main signal.
2. EloIf both teams match a real senior men's ClubElo rating, an Elo-based Poisson model contributes 20%. If it disagrees with the market by more than 25 points it is discarded.
3. PoissonGoal rates are fitted to the blended probabilities (Dixon-Coles low-score correction) to derive Over 2.5 / BTTS. These use a fixed 2.65 total-goals assumption, so treat them as rough.
4. AccasLegs need odds, a market-based probability and a win chance of 60% or more. The highest-probability legs are combined into 2–6 leg accas.
